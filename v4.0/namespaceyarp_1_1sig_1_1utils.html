◆ mix()

Sound yarp::sig::utils::mix ( const Sound &  A,
const Sound &  B,
double  percentage 
)

Mixes two sounds together into a single sound.

This function combines the audio of two sounds with the same number of channels and sample rate. If the sounds have different durations, silence will be added to the shorter one to match the length of the longer sound before mixing.

The percentage parameter controls the relative loudness of the two sounds in the mix. For example, if percentage is 0.7, the resulting sound will consist of 70% of the first sound (A) and 30% of the second sound (B).

The resulting mixed sound is returned as a new Sound object. The original sounds (A and B) are not modified.

Parameters
AThe first sound to mix.
BThe second sound to mix.
percentageThe weight of the first sound in the mix (between 0.0 and 1.0). The second sound will have a weight of (1 - percentage).
Returns
A new Sound object containing the mixed audio.
Exceptions
std::runtime_errorIf the sounds have different sample rates or channel counts.
